HOW TO PLAY

Goal. Survive wave after wave of living chess pieces on the neon board. Every enemy moves by the real rules of chess — learn their steps and stay out of the squares they threaten.

Controls. WASD / arrows move · mouse aims · left click shoots · Space dash · Shift sprint · 1–5 / Q switch weapon · Esc pause · R restart on the game-over screen. On touch screens use the two sticks and the DASH button.

The pieces.
♟Pawn — steps one square, lunges diagonally. Weak alone, deadly in a swarm.
♞Knight — leaps in an L, jumping over everything. Watch the landing square.
♝Bishop — streaks along diagonals and fires diagonal bolts.
♜Rook — a tank that charges down ranks and files.
♛Queen — elite: moves and shoots every way at once.
♚King — the boss, every 10th wave. Three phases, summons guards, paints the board in danger.

Progression. Kills drop XP gems — level up and pick 1 of 3 upgrades. Elites and the King drop new weapons: Rapid Laser, Shotgun, Piercing Railgun and the Explosive Launcher. Clear a wave to heal a little.
